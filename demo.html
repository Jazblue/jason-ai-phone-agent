Frequently Asked Questions

What is an AI phone receptionist?

An AI phone receptionist is software that answers inbound and outbound phone calls using natural, conversational AI voice. It handles calls 24/7, qualifies leads, answers FAQs, books appointments, and escalates to human agents when needed — without any human picking up the phone.

Does this use a real UK phone number?

Yes. A Twilio UK phone number was selected, the regulatory bundle was submitted and approved, and the number was connected to the Vapi AI assistant. Real inbound calls from mobile phones and real outbound calls to mobile phones were both tested and verified successfully.

Can the AI answer calls and schedule appointments?

The current proof of concept demonstrates conversational call handling with the Vapi assistant. Calendar integration and automated appointment booking are planned as future AWS backend extensions, along with call logging, DynamoDB records, and human escalation workflows.

What technology powers this AI phone agent?

The system uses Vapi for AI voice orchestration and Twilio for programmable telephony with a UK phone number. AWS backend services including Lambda, API Gateway, and DynamoDB are planned for future integration to add call logging, automation, and scaling capabilities.

Is this a production-ready AI receptionist?

This is a proof of concept demonstrating real telephone calling with AI voice. The core telephony and voice orchestration components are implemented and tested. AWS backend integrations for call logging, calendar booking, and production monitoring are planned for future development.
